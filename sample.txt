Information Technology Mini Project: Huffman Text Compression and Decompression System.
Data compression is the process of reducing the number of bits needed to represent data.
Lossless data compression algorithms ensure that the original data can be perfectly reconstructed.
Huffman coding is a greedy algorithm that assigns variable-length prefix codes to characters.
Characters that appear with higher frequency receive shorter binary codes.
Characters that appear with lower frequency receive longer binary codes.
This demonstration verifies that encoding and decoding are completely lossless!
